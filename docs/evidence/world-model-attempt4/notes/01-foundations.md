## Synthesis
The primary World Models paper supplies the foundational embodied/interactive recipe: compress observations, model action-conditioned latent dynamics recurrently, and optimize a controller using the model, including in imagined rollouts. Dreamer shifts from the original two-stage controller-evolution framing toward gradient-based policy/value optimization through latent imagination. DayDreamer demonstrates the relevance of that line to real physical interaction, while Iso-Dream foregrounds a limitation: not all observed dynamics are controllable by the agent. The 2026 WAM abstract reflects a further emphasis on action-relevant latent structure. These works do not establish that a world model is a universally faithful simulator: evidence is task- and method-specific, and abstracts report author claims. Date metadata in the Dreamer result is the v3 date, not necessarily the original submission/publication date. The World Models full-text fetch was available; other source evidence here is abstract/search-result text.
